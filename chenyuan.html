Chen Yuan 陳昱安

About關於

陳昱安，來自臺灣臺北，高中畢業於臺北市高級中等教育階段非學校型態實驗教育，現就讀國立臺北藝術大學新媒體藝術學系學士在學中。

創作專長橫跨創意海報、動態影像設計、新媒體錄像藝術、AudioVisual、VJ 等，長期關注影像語言與其所構成的敘事結構與創意，在創作中不斷探索媒介邊界與自我敘述之間的張力，並與觀者達到共鳴。

Based in Taipei, Taiwan. A graduate of a non-school-based experimental education program at the senior-secondary level in Taipei, currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in New Media Art at Taipei National University of the Arts.

The practice spans creative posters, motion graphics, new media video art, audiovisual work and VJing, with a long-standing focus on the language of images and the narrative structures and creativity they hold. An ongoing exploration of the tension between the boundaries of media and self-narration, seeking resonance with the viewer.
